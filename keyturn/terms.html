What Keyturn is

An iPhone app that builds a set of widgets for your car screen — the time, the weather, your next calendar event, a photo, a short line of text — in themes and seasonal packs, shows them on CarPlay through iOS widgets, and can play a sound or a spoken greeting through automations you set up in Apple's Shortcuts app when you connect to the car, leave it or arrive home.

Safe driving comes first

  • Set everything up while parked. Build your set, add widgets and create automations only when the vehicle is stopped and it is safe and legal to do so.
  • Do not interact with the app or your iPhone while driving. The widgets are glanceable information, not something to operate on the road.
  • Keyturn is not a navigation, safety, alert or driver-assistance system. It does not know whether the vehicle is moving and does not check whether using a feature is safe or lawful where you are.
  • Obey the law and your car. Follow traffic laws and your vehicle maker's instructions, and keep sound and greeting volume at a level that lets you hear traffic, sirens and warnings.
  • You are solely responsible for how you use the app while in or near a vehicle.

Forecasts can be wrong or out of date, especially when the iPhone has no connection, and the widget may show the last forecast it received. Do not rely on it for decisions about safety, road conditions or travel.

  • How and when widgets appear in your car is decided by iOS, CarPlay and your car's system. Your car or adapter may not support widgets, iOS may refresh them less often than you expect, and updates to iOS or your car can change what is possible.

Sounds, the greeting and Shortcuts

Sounds and the greeting play through automations that you create in Apple's Shortcuts app. iOS decides when an automation runs, may ask for confirmation or show a banner instead, and may change how automations work. We do not guarantee that a sound plays every time. Keyturn never plays sounds or the greeting during a phone call, on purpose.
