Keyturn Support

Something not working, or a question about your subscription? Write to sergiusil@proton.me and say what happened, which iPhone and iOS version you have, and which car (make, model, year) or CarPlay adapter. One person reads that mailbox, and you will normally hear back within two business days.

There is no account and no password, so there is nothing to recover — just email us.

My set doesn't show up in the car

Check these in order — usually one of them is it.

  • The car has to connect once. Your car is listed in CarPlay settings only after it has connected to this iPhone. Plug in with a cable or connect wirelessly, wait for CarPlay to start, then check again.
  • Show Widgets must be on. Open Settings → General → CarPlay → your car → Widgets. If Show Widgets is off, no widgets show up in your car — even ones you added. Using a CarPlay adapter? Tap the adapter's name instead of the car.
  • Add the Keyturn widgets. On the same page, tap Add Widgets, search for "Keyturn" and add Car Screen 1, Car Screen 2 and so on, in order. Each Car Screen widget shows one tile from your set.
  • Keyturn Pro. Without Pro, the car shows a locked preview instead of your set.
  • After a restart, unlock your iPhone once. Widgets can't read their data until you do — then they fill in on their own.
  • Smart Rotate and Widget Suggestions can swap your widgets for others; turn them off on the same page if you want your set to stay put.

If your car's CarPlay settings have no Widgets page at all, that car or adapter does not show widgets, and Keyturn cannot change that. Still stuck? Use Still nothing? Tell us about your car in the app, or email us with your car's details.

The weather is missing or wrong

  • Location off? Keyturn needs location only while the app is open, and only approximately. Turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Keyturn, or pick your city by hand in the app.
  • Out of date? The widget refreshes the forecast about once an hour when there is a connection. Without one, it shows the last forecast it received.
  • Weather is provided by Apple Weather — it is a forecast, not a guarantee.

My next event is missing

  • Turn on calendar access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → Keyturn.
  • The widget shows timed events from now until the end of tomorrow. All-day events are left out on purpose.
  • The event must be in a calendar on this iPhone (iCloud, Google or another account added in Settings → Apps → Calendar).

A sound or the greeting doesn't play

iOS lets Shortcuts — not apps — start a sound when you connect to the car, leave it or get home, so the sound plays through an automation you set up once in the Shortcuts app. The guide in Keyturn walks you through it.

  • Is the automation on? In Shortcuts → Automation, check that it isn't switched off.
  • Run Immediately. Open the automation: with Run After Confirmation, iOS asks first — and nothing plays while you drive. Choose Run Immediately, and turn off Notify When Run if you get a banner instead of the sound.
  • Play Sound after Keyturn. The automation needs the Keyturn action (Play Keyturn Sound or Play Keyturn Greeting) followed by Play Sound. Keyturn hands over the sound; Play Sound plays it.
  • Test it once. In Shortcuts, tap the shortcut to run it. When it plays, the app shows "Working".
  • During a phone call Keyturn never plays sounds or the greeting — on purpose. It plays next time.
  • Too quiet? The sound plays through your car speakers, under your music. Turn up the car volume.

Managing your subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. We never see your card.

  • To see, change or cancel it: open the Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Keyturn. Cancelling stops the next renewal and leaves access until the paid period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Free trial: to avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. The app shows the date the trial ends.
  • Lifetime: a one-time purchase that never renews. Buying it does not cancel a subscription you already have — cancel that yourself as above.
  • To restore a purchase on a new device or after reinstalling, open the app → Settings → Restore Purchases. The same Apple ID must be signed in.
  • Refunds are issued by Apple, not by us: go to https://reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.

If a purchase went through and Pro did not unlock, try Restore Purchases first, then write to us — include the Apple receipt so we can find it.

Your data

Your calendar, photos, sounds and settings stay on your iPhone; your approximate location goes only to Apple, for the forecast and your city's name. There is no account, and deleting the app erases everything it kept. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy: https://great-apps-s.github.io/keyturn/privacy
