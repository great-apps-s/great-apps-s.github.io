Privacy Policy

Keyturn: Car Dashboard Widgets — published by Sergiu Silosiev

Effective 24 October 2026

Keyturn puts a set of widgets on your car screen through CarPlay — the time, the weather where you are, your next event, a photo, a short line of text — and can play a sound or a spoken greeting through Shortcuts when you get in or out of the car. To do that it reads a few personal things: where you roughly are, what is next in your calendar, the photos you choose. This page says exactly what happens to each of them.

Short version: there is no account, and we run no server that receives your data. Your calendar, photos, sounds and settings stay on your iPhone. Your approximate location goes only to Apple, to get the forecast and the name of your city. The one outside service the app talks to besides Apple is Adapty, which handles the subscription and never sees your calendar, photos or location.

There is no account

Keyturn has no sign-up and no login. We never ask for your email address, phone number or date of birth, and we have no way to link what the app does to who you are.

Location and weather

Why the app asks. The weather widget shows the temperature and sky where you are. iOS asks you once whether Keyturn may use your location while the app is in use. Saying no is fine: you can type a city instead, and the app works without either.

How precise. Keyturn asks iOS only for an approximate location (accurate to a few kilometres), even if you allow precise location. It never asks for location in the background.

Where it goes. To show the forecast, the app — and its widget, when iOS asks it to refresh — sends the approximate coordinates to Apple Weather (Apple's WeatherKit service). To show your city's name, it sends them to Apple Maps. If you type a city instead, the letters you type go to Apple Maps to suggest matching cities. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); Apple states that WeatherKit uses location only to provide the forecast and does not link it to you. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather; its data sources are listed at https://weatherkit.apple.com/legal-attribution.html.

What we do not do. We never receive your location. It is not sent to Adapty, not used for advertising, not used to track where you drive, and no history of places is kept.

What stays on the iPhone. The last place used for the weather (its approximate coordinates and the city name) and the latest forecast are saved on your iPhone so that the widget on your car screen can show and refresh the weather without opening the app. Each update replaces the previous one.

You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Keyturn, or pick a city by hand in the app.

Your calendar

Why the app asks. The "next event" widget shows something like "Dentist in 35 min". iOS only lets an app read calendar events with full calendar access, so that is what iOS asks you for.

What the app reads. Upcoming events with a start time (not all-day events) from now until the end of tomorrow. It keeps only each event's title and start time — not attendees, notes, locations or other calendars' details — so the widget can show the next one. That short list is replaced every time the app refreshes it.

What it does not do. The calendar never leaves your iPhone: it is not sent to us, to Adapty or to anyone else. Keyturn never creates, changes or deletes an event.

Saying no is fine — your set simply skips the next event. Change it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → Keyturn.

Photos

Keyturn uses the system photo picker. The app has no access to your photo library: it receives only the photos you pick, one choice at a time.

Each photo you pick is copied, reduced to a small size for the car screen, and saved inside the app's storage on your iPhone, where the widget can show it. The copy is a new picture: it does not carry over details of the original such as where it was taken. Your original stays in Photos, untouched. Photos are never uploaded anywhere and are never used to train anything.

The copies stay in the app's storage on your iPhone until you delete the app, which removes all of them. Pictures from Keyturn's own theme packs are part of the app.

Sounds, the greeting and Shortcuts

Sounds you import. If you add your own sound from the Files app, Keyturn copies that one file into its storage on your iPhone. It is not uploaded anywhere.

The greeting. The spoken greeting is put together from the time, the weather, your next event and, if you type one, your first name. The voice is the iPhone's own built-in voice: the speech is made on the device and saved as a short audio file there. Your first name is used only for the greeting and stays on the iPhone.

Shortcuts. Sounds and the greeting play through automations that you create in Apple's Shortcuts app; Keyturn hands the sound to Shortcuts and Shortcuts plays it. To show you whether an automation worked, the app keeps on the device a short note of when it last ran.

Phone calls and the car. So that nothing plays over a phone call, Keyturn checks whether a call is in progress — a yes or no, nothing about who is calling. While the app is open, it also checks whether your iPhone's sound is going to a car, to show the right setup step. Neither is recorded or sent anywhere.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

To let the widgets on your car screen and the Shortcuts actions work while the app is closed, Keyturn keeps its data in a storage area on your iPhone that only the app and its own widget and actions can read. It is protected by iOS file encryption. It holds: your widget sets, themes and display settings; the photos, sounds and greeting described above; the last place and forecast; the upcoming-event list; whether Pro is unlocked; and a few markers such as when the widget last refreshed.

In ordinary app settings the app also remembers things like whether you finished onboarding, when it was installed, whether it already asked you for a rating, your answer to "do you see it in your car?", and when the limited-time lifetime offer was first shown.

None of this leaves the device, except as described under Location and weather and Subscriptions and payments.

Subscriptions and payments

Purchases are made through Apple. We never see your card number, and Apple does not give it to us.

Subscription status and the subscription screens are managed for us by Adapty (https://adapty.io/privacy). So that a purchase is recognised, restored after a reinstall and shown on the right screen, Adapty receives:

  • the App Store purchase receipt and purchase history;
  • a random identifier that Adapty creates for this installation — it identifies the installation, not you;
  • the identifier iOS gives to apps from one publisher (IDFV), the device model, iOS version, language, region, time zone and app version;
  • when the app is opened and which subscription screens were shown, so that we can see which offer works.

We have configured Adapty not to collect the advertising identifier (IDFA) and not to record your IP address in the subscription profile. Adapty receives no location, no calendar, no photos and no sounds. We do not give Adapty your name or email — we do not have them.

Apple's own handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Keyturn contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no attribution SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites and we do not use the advertising identifier, so the app never shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt — there is nothing to ask permission for. We do not sell or share personal information.

If you have turned on "Share With App Developers" in iOS, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics under Apple's terms; you can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

Because there is no account and no copy of your data on any server of ours, there is nothing to request: deleting Keyturn from your iPhone erases everything it kept — sets, photo copies, sounds, the greeting, the saved place, forecast and event list. Remove the Keyturn widgets from your car screen and Home Screen too. Your calendar and your original photos are not touched by deleting the app.

To have the purchase records held by our subscription provider deleted, write to sergiusil@proton.me and say roughly when you bought; we will have Adapty delete that profile and confirm to you within one month. Apple's own records of your purchase are governed by Apple. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription — see the Terms of Use.

Keyturn is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly process the personal data of a child. If you believe a child's data has reached us, write to sergiusil@proton.me and we will delete it.
