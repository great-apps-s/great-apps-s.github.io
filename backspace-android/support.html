Backspace Support

Something not working, or a question about your subscription? Write to gr.apps@zohomail.eu and say what happened, which phone you have and which Android version. One person reads that mailbox, and you will normally hear back within two business days.

There is no account and no password, so there is nothing to recover — just email us.

The scan finds nothing, or too little

  • Check photo access. In Android Settings → Apps → Backspace → Permissions → Photos and videos, choose Always allow all. With access to selected photos only, the app sees only what you picked, and duplicates outside that selection stay hidden. The app's home screen also has a button to allow all photos.
  • Favorites are skipped on purpose. You can change that in the app's Settings, under Protection.
  • Media in the trash is not scanned. Items already in your gallery's trash are on their way out and are left alone.

I deleted something by mistake

Open your gallery app (for example Google Photos → Collections → Trash, or the trash folder of your phone's gallery), select the item and restore it. Android keeps deleted items there for 30 days. After that, or once the trash is emptied, they cannot be recovered — not by us and not by the app.

The space did not go down after deleting

Deleted items stay in the trash for 30 days and keep taking space until then. Use Remove from trash now after deleting in the app, or empty the trash in your gallery, to free the space straight away.

A video would not shrink

Some videos — for example ones in formats your phone cannot re-encode — are skipped. Shrinking long 4K videos takes a while; keep the app open, and allow notifications to see progress. If an ordinary video still fails, email us its length and the approximate time you tried.

Contacts

Merging and deleting contacts cannot be undone in the app. Make a backup in Tidy contacts → Backups before merging; you can restore missing cards from it at any time. If a card stays after deleting or merging, it belongs to an account that does not accept changes from the phone (some messengers and work accounts): the app cannot remove it, and when merging it links it to the kept card instead.

The private vault

The vault opens with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. It is stored only in the app on this phone: it is not in phone backups, it does not move to a new phone, and deleting the app deletes it. Take items out before you change phones or remove the app.

The speaker tone

Disconnect headphones and Bluetooth speakers first — the tone has to come out of the phone itself. Hold the phone speaker-side down and let the tone run to the end; repeat if the sound is still muffled. It is not a repair: if the phone was submerged or the sound does not come back, contact your phone's manufacturer or an authorised service provider.

Managing your subscription

  • Cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Backspace. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Restore a purchase after reinstalling or on a new phone: open the subscription screen or Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Google account you bought with.
  • Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policies — request one through Google Play, or write to us and we will help.

Your data
