What Backspace is

An app that helps you free up space on your Android phone. It scans your photo library on the device, groups duplicates, similar shots, blurry photos, screenshots, large videos and media saved by messengers, lets you remove what you choose, shrinks videos and converts photos you choose, shows photos taken on this day in earlier years, tidies your contacts, keeps private items in a locked vault, shows how much space your apps take, checks your phone's hardware, measures your internet speed, and plays a tone through the speaker to help push water or dust out of it.

You decide what is deleted

The app suggests; you decide. Nothing is deleted until you confirm it, and Android asks you to confirm again.

  • Check before you confirm. Grouping similar shots and choosing the "best" one is automatic and can be wrong. A pre-selected item is a suggestion, not a judgement that you do not need it.
  • Deleted items go to the trash of your gallery, where Android keeps them for 30 days. After that, or once you empty the trash, they are gone and cannot be recovered by us or by the app.
  • Keep a backup of anything you cannot afford to lose. The app is a tool for removing files, and removal is its purpose.

You are responsible for what you choose to delete. We are not liable for the loss of photos or videos you deleted after confirming, including once the trash has been emptied.

Shrinking videos and lighter photos

Shrinking replaces a video with a smaller copy, and converting replaces a JPEG photo with a smaller HEIC copy. The copies keep the original date, place and favorite mark, but a shrunk video has lower quality — fewer pixels and a lower bitrate — and the saving shown before you start is an estimate. The original goes to the trash and can be restored from there for 30 days; after that, only the copy remains. Some videos cannot be shrunk.

Contacts

Merging duplicate contacts and deleting cards cannot be undone in the app, and the change reaches every device that syncs the same account. Merging keeps one card and moves the other cards' details into it. Make a backup first — the app offers one for free — and keep it until you are sure.

The private vault

The vault is stored only in the app on this phone, encrypted with a key that cannot leave the phone. It is not included in phone backups and does not move to a new phone, and deleting the app deletes the vault and everything in it. Take out anything you want to keep before you remove the app or change phones. If you lose access to your phone's screen lock, you may lose access to the vault.

Apps

Uninstalling apps from the Apps screen is done through Android's own dialog. Removing an app may delete its data; the app shows you what it knows, but you decide what to remove.

The speaker tone

The speaker tool plays a loud low tone for a short time. It may help move water or dust out of the speaker grille; it is not a repair, it does not treat water damage, and we do not promise any result. Do not hold the phone to your ear while it plays, and do not use it with headphones. If the sound stays muffled or the phone was submerged, contact your phone's manufacturer or an authorised service provider.

Phone check

The phone check is an aid, not a certified diagnosis. Some checks depend on what your phone allows the app to see, and some rely on what you report. The PDF report records the results and when the checks were done; it is not a guarantee of the phone's condition.
