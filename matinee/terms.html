What Matinee is

An iPhone video player for media you already have: videos on your iPhone, your own Plex, Jellyfin or Emby server, IPTV playlists and Xtream logins you have from a provider, with an optional TV guide (XMLTV), and folders on your network (SMB, WebDAV). It plays them on your iPhone and, while the car is parked, on your car's screen through CarPlay. It can download videos for watching without a connection and has a kids mode locked with a PIN.

Safe driving comes first

  • Video on the car's screen is for when the car is parked. The driver must never watch video while driving, and must not operate the app or the iPhone while the car is moving.
  • When the car moves, iOS and your car switch Matinee to sound only, and video returns when you park. Matinee itself does not detect whether the car is moving and cannot guarantee this switch in every car; it relies on iOS and the car. If video ever shows while you drive, do not watch it — stop playback or pull over safely.
  • Set up sources and choose what to watch before you drive.
  • Obey the law and your car. Follow traffic laws — some places restrict any screen the driver can see — and your vehicle maker's instructions. Keep the volume at a level that lets you hear traffic, sirens and warnings.
  • Matinee is not a navigation, safety or driver-assistance system. You are solely responsible for how you use the app while in or near a vehicle.

Changes to those services can stop a source from working in Matinee, and we cannot guarantee that any of them stays compatible.
