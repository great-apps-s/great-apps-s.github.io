Privacy Policy

Eily for Android — published by GREAT APPS

Effective 23 November 2026

Eily is a meditation and relaxation app: guided meditations and courses, breathing exercises, sleep and relaxation sounds, music and affirmations. When you first open it, a short questionnaire asks about your goals and how you feel — stress, worry, sadness, your mood, your relationships, your physical well-being. Those answers are personal, so this page says exactly what happens to them, and to everything else the app touches on Android.

Short version: there is no account, and your questionnaire answers stay on your phone — they are not sent to us, and they are not sent to analytics. The app tells Amplitude, our analytics service, which screens are opened and what is started; purchases go through Google Play and Adapty; meditations, music and videos are downloaded from Google's Firebase Storage and our own file server. There are no ads, we do not use the advertising ID, and we never sell or share personal information.

Eily is not medical care. It offers relaxation and self-care content for general well-being. It does not diagnose or treat anything and is not a substitute for a doctor, psychologist or therapist.

If you are in crisis — thinking about harming yourself or someone else, or in danger right now — please don't wait for an app. Call your local emergency number (112 in the EU, 911 in the US and Canada, 999 in the UK) or go to the nearest emergency department. In the US you can call or text 988; in the UK and Ireland, Samaritans are on 116 123. Helplines in other countries: https://findahelpline.com

There is no account

Eily has no sign-up and no login. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth. The questionnaire asks for an age range; like every other answer, it stays on your phone.

Your questionnaire answers stay on your phone

When you finish the questionnaire, the app keeps your answers — the questions and what you chose, as text — in its private storage on your phone, where other apps cannot read them. They are used only on the phone.

The app does not send them anywhere — not to us, not to Amplitude, not to Adapty, not to any AI service. Analytics records only that you started, skipped or finished the questionnaire and which step you reached — the steps are counted the same way whatever you answer — and never what you chose.

Not in Android backup. The answers are kept in a separate file that Android's backup and transfer to a new phone leave out, so they never reach your Google account either. If backup is on, Android backs up only Eily's progress and settings — your streak, recently opened items, your sound mix, whether you have finished the questionnaire — not your answers. After a reinstall or on a new phone, the answers are gone.

If Eily ever offers a feature that needs your answers to leave the phone, the app will ask you first, and this policy will describe it before the feature is released.

Analytics (Amplitude)

To understand which parts of Eily are used and where people get stuck, the app sends usage events to Amplitude, an analytics service, which stores them in its EU data centre.

What is sent:

  • the screens you open and close, and which screen you came from;
  • what you start: a breathing exercise, a course or lesson, an affirmation set (with its name or ID), the questionnaire music turned on or off, notifications allowed or not, a reminder opened;
  • that you started, skipped or finished the questionnaire, and which step you reached — never your answers;
  • purchase events: which plan, whether it started a free trial, on which screen — and, if a purchase fails, the technical error code and message;
  • when an app session starts and ends.

Sent with each event: a random analytics ID created by the app (not derived from your phone or your Google account) and a session number; the device brand, manufacturer and model, Android version, app version, language, and the country your phone reports from its settings or mobile network.

Your IP address. The app does not send your IP address to Amplitude, and it does not send a city, region, coordinates or mobile carrier. But like any internet connection, the request itself comes from your IP address, and we do not control whether Amplitude's servers use the address of the connection to work out an approximate location (such as country or city). So we treat an approximate location as something Amplitude may record.

Not sent: your questionnaire answers; the advertising ID and the Android App Set ID (both switched off); your precise location (the app has no location permission); crash reports or Amplitude's own diagnostics (switched off); taps and screen contents collected automatically (automatic collection is limited to app sessions and cannot be widened remotely); your name, email or anything that identifies you directly.

We use these records only to understand and improve the app. We do not use them for advertising, do not build profiles for anyone else and do not sell them. Amplitude processes them for us under its privacy policy: https://amplitude.com/privacy

Subscriptions and payments

Purchases are made through Google Play. We never see your card number, and Google does not give it to us.

Purchases and access are managed for us by Adapty (https://adapty.io/privacy). So that a purchase is recognised, restored after a reinstall and shown correctly, Adapty receives:

  • your Google Play purchase records for Eily (which product, when, the order number and purchase token);
  • identifiers that Android gives this installation — the Android ID and the App Set ID — and an installation ID created by Adapty;
  • the device model, Android version, language, region, time zone, screen size, app version, and the Google Play install referrer (a short note of which store page or link led to the install);
  • when the app is opened, and its requests for the plans to show.

We have configured Adapty not to collect the advertising ID and not to record your IP address in the purchase profile. Adapty receives nothing from your questionnaire, your progress or what you listen to.

Google's own handling of your purchase is covered by Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Meditations, music and videos you play

Some sounds and videos are built into the app. The rest is downloaded when you play it:

  • meditations, courses, breathing guides, calm music, noise and sleep sounds and background videos come from Firebase Storage, a Google Cloud service that hosts these files for us;
  • relaxation sounds for the mixer, relaxing music and a background video come from our own file server (api.great-s-apps.store), which sits behind Cloudflare.

These are ordinary downloads of the same public files for everyone. A request contains the address of the file and what any download from a phone carries — your IP address and, in a technical header, the phone's model and Android version. It contains no account, no app identifier and nothing about you. Our file server keeps no log of these requests. Google and Cloudflare handle them as our hosting providers, under their own terms: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

What you have played is kept on your phone — streamed files in a cache of up to 1 GB, oldest first out — so it plays again without downloading.

Notifications

On Android 13 and later the app asks for permission to send notifications after you tap Continue on the welcome screen. If notifications are allowed, it schedules on the phone a daily affirmation at about 9:00 for 21 days and, if you have not subscribed, a few reminders about Eily's plans in your first ten days. If you start the Weekly plan's free trial, the app also schedules one reminder for the day before the trial ends; when it is due, the app checks with Adapty that the subscription is still set to renew and skips the reminder if you have cancelled. They are scheduled by Android itself: there is no notification server and no push token, and nothing about you is sent to schedule them. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings. While a meditation or sound plays, Android shows a player notification with pause and play.

Other permissions and sensors

  • Background audio. Meditations, music and sounds keep playing when you leave the app or lock the screen; Android requires a "media playback" service and a player notification for this.
  • Shake. On the Today screen the app may invite you to shake the phone; it reads the motion sensor only while that invitation is on screen, and only on the phone.
  • Vibration gives a light tap when you choose an answer or press a button.
  • The app has no access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files, and does not read health data from other apps.

Deleting your data

On your phone. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Eily → Storage → Clear storage (on some phones: Clear data) to erase your answers, progress, settings and downloaded files. Deleting the app erases them too. Neither touches your purchases, which stay with your Google account.

In your Android backup. If backup was on, the copy of Eily's progress and settings (never your answers) is managed in your Google account — on the phone under Settings → Google → Backup, or at https://one.google.com/storage under device backups.

At Adapty. To have your purchase profile deleted, write to gr.apps@zohomail.eu with the Google Play order number from your receipt email (it starts with GPA.), or roughly when you bought; we will delete it and confirm within one month. Google's own records of your purchases are governed by Google. Deleting data does not cancel a subscription — see the Terms of Use.

At Amplitude. Usage events are filed under a random analytics ID that is not connected to your name, email, Google account or purchase, so there is nothing you could send us that would let us find yours. After you clear the app's storage or reinstall it, the app starts with a new analytics ID. If you want us to try anyway, write to us and we will do what we can.
