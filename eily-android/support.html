Eily Support

Something not working, or a question about your purchase? Write to gr.apps@zohomail.eu and tell us what happened, which phone and Android version you have, roughly when it happened and on which screen. We read every message.

In the app, tap ⓘ on the Today screen → Contact support: it opens your email app addressed to us, with the app version in the subject. There is no Eily account and no password, so there is nothing to recover. Please don't send us your questionnaire answers or anything about your health — we don't need them to help you.

Eily can't help in an emergency, and this mailbox is not watched around the clock. If you are thinking about harming yourself or someone else, or are in danger right now, call your local emergency number (112 in the EU, 911 in the US and Canada, 999 in the UK) or go to the nearest emergency department. In the US you can call or text 988; in the UK and Ireland, Samaritans are on 116 123. Helplines in other countries: https://findahelpline.com

No sound

  • Turn up the media volume with the volume buttons while something is playing — the ringer volume and silent mode don't control it.
  • Check where the sound goes: a connected Bluetooth speaker or headphones take it over. Disconnecting headphones pauses playback on purpose; tap play again.
  • A phone call, an alarm or another app playing sound pauses Eily. After a short interruption it carries on by itself; otherwise tap play.
  • The music on the questionnaire screens has its own sound button at the top of the screen.
  • If a meditation or sound stops when the screen turns off, your phone may be restricting Eily in the background: open your phone's Settings → Apps → Eily → Battery and choose Unrestricted (the name differs between phone makers).

No internet, or it won't load

Many meditations, courses, sounds and videos are streamed the first time you play them, so they need a connection.

  • If something doesn't start or says it couldn't load, check your Wi-Fi or mobile data and try again.
  • Anything you have played before is kept on your phone and plays again without a connection, until the phone needs the space back (the cache holds up to 1 GB).
  • The questionnaire, the short breathing exercise right after it and the built-in ocean background work offline.

Reminders and notifications

  • No affirmations or reminders (including the reminder before a free trial ends)? Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Eily → Notifications and turn them on. On Android 13 and later the app asks once, after the first Continue.
  • Reminders arrive at about the time set, not to the minute: Android may shift them a little to save battery.
  • To stop them, turn notifications off in the same place.

Subscriptions, Lifetime and the free trial

Purchases are handled by Google Play, not by us. We never see your card.

  • To see, change or cancel a subscription: in Eily tap ⓘ on the Today screen → Manage subscription, or open the Google Play app → tap your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Eily. Cancelling stops the next renewal and leaves access until the paid period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Free trial: the Weekly plan may start with 3 free days, if you have not subscribed to Eily before. Cancel in Google Play before the trial ends and you are not charged; otherwise the weekly subscription starts when the trial ends. If notifications are allowed, the app reminds you the day before the trial ends (unless you have already cancelled).
  • Lifetime is a one-time purchase that never renews. Buying it does not cancel a subscription you already have — cancel that yourself as above.
  • To restore a purchase on a new phone or after reinstalling: tap ⓘ on the Today screen → Restore purchases, or Restore on any plan screen. The same Google account must be signed in to Google Play.
  • Refunds go through Google Play. Shortly after a purchase you can request one yourself from your order history — see https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637. After that, email us the order number and we will look at it.

If a purchase went through and nothing unlocked, try Restore purchases first, then write to us — include the Google Play order number from your receipt (it starts with GPA.) so we can find it.

Your answers and your data

Your questionnaire answers stay on your phone: they are not sent to us or to analytics. To erase them, together with your progress, settings and downloaded files, open your phone's Settings → Apps → Eily → Storage → Clear storage, or delete the app; your purchases stay with your Google account. Your answers are not included in Android's backup to your Google account; only your progress and settings are, if backup is on. To have your purchase profile deleted, email us your Google Play order number. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy: https://great-apps-s.github.io/eily-android/privacy
