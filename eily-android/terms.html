What Eily is

An Android app for relaxation and everyday well-being: guided meditations and courses, breathing exercises, sleep and relaxation sounds you can mix, music, affirmations and short articles. A short questionnaire at the start asks about your goals and how you feel; your answers stay on your phone. Many meditations, sounds and videos are streamed over the internet the first time you play them.

Use it safely

  • Breathing exercises, and any movement or yoga sessions, are physical activity. Stop if you feel dizzy, short of breath, faint or in pain. If you are pregnant, or have a heart, lung, blood-pressure, back or other condition, ask a doctor before you start.
  • Don't listen while driving or doing anything that needs your full attention: meditations and sleep sounds are made to relax you and can make you drowsy.
  • Keep the volume at a comfortable level, especially with headphones and when falling asleep.
