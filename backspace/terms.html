What Backspace is

An app that helps you free up space on your iPhone. It scans your photo library on the device, groups duplicates, similar shots, screenshots and large videos, lets you remove what you choose, shrinks videos you choose, shows photos taken on this day in earlier years so you can keep or remove them, and plays a tone through the speaker to help push water out of it.

You decide what is deleted

The app suggests; you decide. Nothing is deleted until you confirm it, and iOS asks you to confirm again.

  • Check before you confirm. Grouping similar shots and choosing the "best" one is automatic and can be wrong. A pre-selected item is a suggestion, not a judgement that you do not need it.
  • Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where iOS keeps them for up to 30 days. After that, or once you empty that album, they are gone and cannot be recovered by us or by the app.
  • With iCloud Photos on, a deletion also removes the item from your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID.
  • Keep a backup of anything you cannot afford to lose. The app is a tool for removing files, and removal is its purpose.

You are responsible for what you choose to delete. We are not liable for the loss of photos or videos you deleted after confirming, including once Recently Deleted has been emptied.

Shrinking videos

Shrinking replaces a video with a smaller copy. The copy keeps the original date and place, but it has lower quality — fewer pixels and a lower bitrate — and the estimated saving shown before you start is an estimate. The original goes to Recently Deleted and can be restored from there for up to 30 days; after that, only the smaller copy remains. Some videos (for example slow-motion or edited ones) cannot be shrunk.

The speaker tone

The speaker tool plays a loud low tone for a short time. It may help move water out of the speaker grille; it is not a repair, it does not treat water damage, and we do not promise any result. Do not hold the phone to your ear while it plays, and do not use it with headphones — the app refuses to start while headphones are connected. If the sound stays muffled or the phone was submerged, contact Apple or an authorised service provider.
