Privacy Policy

Heirloom — published by Sergiu Silosiev

Effective 14 September 2026

Heirloom shows what a couple's future child might look like, and how a face might age. To do that it needs photographs, which are the most personal thing this app touches. This page says exactly what happens to them.

Short version: we do not keep your photos. They are sent once to the service that generates the picture and are deleted right after it — not stored by us, not used to train anything, and never sold or shared with anyone else.

Who we are

Sergiu Silosiev (Sergiu Silosiev) is the publisher of Heirloom and the data controller for the processing described here.

Questions, requests, complaints: sergiusil@proton.me.

There is no account

Heirloom has no sign-up and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth or address, and we have no way to link what the app does to who you are.

The photos you choose

What happens. You pick photographs from your photo library, or take one with the camera. The app reads them into memory and sends them, together with a text instruction, to our image-generation provider, which returns the generated picture. That is the whole journey.

What we do not do with them:

  • we do not store them on any server of ours;
  • we do not use them to train any model;
  • we do not sell them, rent them out, or share them with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else;
  • we do not attempt to identify anyone in them (see Face data and biometrics below).

Where they go. Generation is performed on our behalf by a third-party image-generation provider. Your photograph travels inside a single request to it, is used to produce the picture, and that is the end of its journey. The provider acts on our instructions and is not permitted to use your photographs for its own purposes.

How long it is kept. Only for as long as the picture takes to make. We send every generation request with storage switched off at the provider, so your photograph is not written into any request history, and the finished picture is removed from the provider's delivery network within minutes of the app downloading it. Nothing about the photograph is kept afterwards.

Permission to upload someone else. The second photograph is usually of your partner. Before you can create anything, the app asks you to confirm that the person in it agreed. That confirmation is yours to give truthfully — we cannot check it, and it is the basis on which their photograph is processed.

Face data and biometrics

This is the section people in this category most want an answer to, so it is explicit.

Heirloom does not perform face recognition and does not collect biometric data. In particular:

  • we do not create, derive, extract or store a faceprint, a face template, a face embedding, or a scan of face geometry;
  • we do not detect, measure or index facial features — the app contains no face-detection and no face-recognition code at all;
  • we do not use a photograph to identify anyone, to verify anyone's identity, or to match a face against any database, ours or anyone else's;
  • we do not sell, lease, trade, licence or otherwise disclose face data to anyone, and we never profit from it.

What actually happens is simpler than that: the whole photograph is handed to an image model, which draws a new picture. Nothing is measured, nothing is extracted, and nothing about a face is kept afterwards.

We therefore do not collect "biometric identifiers" or "biometric information" as those terms are used in the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act, the Texas Capture or Use of Biometric Identifier Act, or the Washington biometric privacy law, and we do not collect "sensitive personal information" of that kind under California law.

The pictures the app creates

Generated pictures are written to your device only, inside the app's own storage. They are not uploaded anywhere.

  • Deleting a picture in the app deletes it, together with every age variant generated from it.
  • Deleting the app removes all of them at once.
  • If you tap Save, the picture is copied to your photo library. The app can add photographs to your library, and cannot read, change or delete what is already there.

What the app remembers about itself

On your device, in ordinary app settings: whether you finished onboarding, whether the one free generation has been used, whether the promo offer and the rating prompt have been shown, your subscription status, and the list of pictures you have created. Nothing in that list identifies you, and none of it leaves the device.

Subscriptions and payments

Purchases are made through Apple. We never see your card number, and Apple does not give it to us.

Subscription state is managed for us by Adapty (https://adapty.io/privacy), which receives the purchase receipt and an identifier generated for the installation — not for you — so that a subscription bought on one device is recognised on another. It receives no photographs and no generated pictures.

Apple's own handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Heirloom contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no attribution SDK. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, we do not use the advertising identifier, and the app therefore never asks for tracking permission. There is no profile of you anywhere, because we do not build one.

Deleting your data

Photographs you upload are deleted as soon as the picture is made — you do not have to ask, and there is nothing left to request.

Everything else is in your hands, because there is no account: delete a picture inside the app, or remove the app to erase everything it kept.

If you want the purchase records held by our subscription provider removed, write to sergiusil@proton.me and say which purchase; we will pass the request on. Apple's own records of your purchase are governed by Apple.

Children

Heirloom is not directed to children and is not intended for them. We do not knowingly process the personal data of a child. Do not upload photographs of children. If you believe a child's photograph has been used, write to sergiusil@proton.me and we will act on it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to object to or restrict its processing, to receive it in portable form, and to complain to your data protection authority. In California you additionally have the right to know, to delete, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information — we do not sell or share personal information, and never have.

Write to sergiusil@proton.me. Note what we said above: without an account we usually hold nothing that identifies you, and the honest answer to "what do you have on me" is normally "nothing".

Legal basis (for users in the EEA and the UK)

We process your photographs to perform the service you asked for, on your instruction, and — for the photograph of another person — on the basis of the consent you confirm before generating. Purchase data is processed to perform the subscription contract and to meet our legal obligations.
