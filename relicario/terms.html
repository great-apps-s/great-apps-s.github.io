What Relicario is

An iPhone app for old family photographs. You photograph paper prints with the camera, or pick pictures from Photos, and keep them in an archive on your iPhone. When you ask, the app sends a photo to be restored with AI — damage repaired, faded or black-and-white photos colorized, the picture enlarged — and can make a family portrait: a loved one, cut out of their own photo, placed into a family photo. Restoration needs an internet connection and is done by AI models run by third-party services, named in the Privacy Policy.

Results are AI reconstructions, not records

  • A restored photo is drawn by an AI model. What is missing or damaged in the original — parts of a face, details, colours — is reconstructed, not recovered. The colours of a black-and-white photo are an estimate. A result can differ from the original, and from how the person really looked.
  • A family portrait is a composite: the person you add was not in that photograph.
  • Look at each result next to the original before you save or share it. The app shows them side by side and asks whether the person looks like themselves; if not, tap Not quite for a gentle retry from the original.
  • Do not use results as identification, in official or legal documents, as evidence, or as proof of historical or genealogical fact. Do not present a result — especially a family portrait — as an unaltered photograph where that could mislead someone.

Cuando lo pides, la app envía una foto para restaurarla con IA — repara daños, da color a fotos desvaídas o en blanco y negro, amplía la imagen — y puede crear un retrato familiar: un ser querido, recortado de su propia foto y colocado en una foto de familia.
