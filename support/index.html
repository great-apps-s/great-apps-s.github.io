Heirloom Support

Something not working, or a question about your subscription? Write to sergiusil@proton.me and say what happened and which device you are on. One person reads that mailbox, and you will normally hear back within two business days.

There is no account and no password, so there is nothing to recover — just email us.

The picture did not come out

Generation needs a connection and takes about a minute. If it fails or the result looks wrong:

  • Use clear, front-facing photographs where the whole face is visible. Sunglasses, a hand across the face, heavy shadow or a very small face in the frame all make the result worse.
  • One face per photograph. With several people in the frame the app cannot tell whose face to use.
  • Try again on a stable connection — a request interrupted halfway does not produce a picture.

If it still fails, email us the approximate time you tried and the device model. We can look up what went wrong on our side.

Managing your subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. We never see your card.

  • To see, change or cancel it: open the Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Heirloom. Cancelling stops the next renewal and leaves access until the paid period ends.
  • To restore a purchase on a new device or after reinstalling, open the app and use Restore Purchases. The same Apple ID must be signed in.
  • Refunds are issued by Apple, not by us: go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.

If a purchase went through and access did not appear, try Restore Purchases first, then write to us — include the Apple receipt so we can find it.

Your photographs

The photographs you upload are deleted as soon as the picture is made. They are not kept, not used to train anything and not handed to anyone beyond the service that creates the picture. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Pictures the app has already made are stored on your device. Deleting one in the app deletes it together with every age variant; removing the app removes all of them.

Report a problem with a result

If a generated picture is offensive, or you believe someone uploaded a photograph of you without permission, email sergiusil@proton.me with the details. We answer this kind of report first.
